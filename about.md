I am a Lecturer in Psychology at the University of Hull, where I lead the [CogLang Lab]({{ '/lab/' | relative_url }}). My research spans language processing, learning and memory, respiration and cognition, and individual differences in cognitive performance.

I joined Hull in 2014. Before that, I worked with Yuki Kamide and Christoph Scheepers at the University of Dundee on how people represent motion events during sentence comprehension, and with Gareth Gaskell at the University of York on word learning and memory consolidation. I completed my DPhil in Psychology at the University of Sussex with Alan Garnham, studying perceptuomotor processing in language comprehension. My first degree was in Cognitive Science at the University of Hertfordshire.

I teach research methods, statistics, psychology of language and psychology of AI, and supervise undergraduate and postgraduate research. I am a Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and served as Programme Director for Psychology with Criminology from 2020 to 2024. I also serve as an Academic Editor for *PLOS ONE* and as School AI Champion and Open Science Champion at Hull.
