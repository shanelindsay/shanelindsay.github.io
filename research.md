I lead the [Breathing, Brain, and Cognition Lab]({{ '/lab/' | relative_url }}) at the University of Hull.

## Breathing, brain, and cognition

I study how breathing shapes human perception, attention, and memory, and how respiratory rhythms are coupled to brain activity. We combine laboratory experiments using respiratory recording and eye tracking with analyses of large neuroimaging and physiological cohorts to understand how breathing relates to cognition and health.

- [Does oral breathing disrupt memory consolidation during waking rest? A registered report](https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218251328994) (2025).
- Respiratory phase and visuospatial attention: A replication and extension (Roberts et al., 2026). [PsyArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/n5ha3_v1).
- The impact of oral and nasal breathing on visual search performance: A pre registered replication and extension (Richards et al., under review).

## Sleep and memory

My work on bodily states grew out of research on how sleep and waking rest consolidate newly learned information in memory, using behavioural methods, polysomnography, and neuroimaging.

- [Verbal fluency difficulties in Chinese insomnia patients: Insights from semantic and phonemic tasks](https://doi.org/10.1080/13803395.2025.2572088) (2025).
- [Sleep preserves original and distorted memory traces](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2017.10.005) (2018). [PDF]({{ '/papers/cairney2017.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [Markers of automaticity in sleep associated consolidation of novel words](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2015.03.025) (2015). [PDF]({{ '/papers/Tham_Lindsay_Gaskell.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [Lexical integration of novel words without sleep](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029243) (2013). [PDF]({{ '/papers/Lexical_integration_of_novel_words_without_sleep.pdf' | relative_url }})

## Language and cognition

I have a longstanding interest in word learning, spoken language processing, and how language interacts with visual attention and motor systems. Current doctoral research with Dr Kate Stone examines verbal fluency and cognitive control in bilingual language comprehension.

- [Does fast mapping accelerate lexical integration? Evidence from spoken and written novel words](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2026.104815) (2027). [PDF]({{ '/papers/1-s2.0-S0749596X26000859-main.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [How well do children remember fast mapped words?](https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.70019) (2025).
- [Plasticity of categories in speech perception and production](https://doi.org/10.1080/23273798.2021.2018471) (2022).
- [Event processing in the visual world](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000199) (2016). [PDF]({{ '/papers/Event_processing_in_the_visual_world.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [The selective role of premotor cortex in speech perception](https://doi.org/10.1162/jocn_a_00463) (2013). [PDF]({{ '/papers/jocn_a_00463_1_.pdf' | relative_url }})

## Measurement and research methods

I work on measurement precision, reliability, effect sizes, meta analysis, and publication bias. Alongside this, I study human judgement, trust, and responsibility in AI assisted decision making and statistical analysis.

- [Same data, different analysts](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-024-02101-x) (2025). [PDF]({{ '/papers/Gould_et_al_2025_same_data_different_analysts.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [Data from an international multi centre study of statistics and mathematics anxieties](https://doi.org/10.5334/jopd.80) (2023).
- [Developmental psychologists should care about measurement precision](https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.2321) (2022).
