I lead the [Breathing and Cognition Lab]({{ '/lab/' | relative_url }}) at the University of Hull. My research investigates how human cognition, memory, and attention interact with breathing and bodily states, alongside work on research methods, open science, and AI in psychological research.

## Breathing, bodily state, and cognition

How do the rhythms and pathways of breathing shape the way we attend, remember, and act, and how do cognitive demands in turn alter how we breathe?

In laboratory studies using respiratory recording and eye tracking, we investigate both the route of breathing (nasal versus oral) and how visual perception, attention, and memory encoding vary across the inhalation and exhalation cycle. Alongside experimental work, we analyse large open physiological and neuroimaging cohorts to study individual differences in human breathing patterns, their stability across rest and cognitive tasks, and how respiratory regulation relates to brain activity, sleep, and wellbeing.

- [Does oral breathing disrupt memory consolidation during waking rest? A registered report](https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218251328994) (2025).
- Respiratory phase and visuospatial attention: A replication and extension (Roberts et al., 2026). [PsyArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/n5ha3_v1).
- The impact of oral and nasal breathing on visual search performance: A pre registered replication and extension (Richards et al., under review).

## Measurement, research methods, and AI

A second strand of my work focuses on how psychological and physiological studies can produce more informative and dependable evidence. I work on measurement precision, test retest reliability, effect sizes and dependence in repeated measures designs, meta analysis, and publication bias. This includes developing open methods for validating and calibrating respiratory recordings against reference airflow measures.

Alongside this, I study the psychology of AI and how AI tools are changing scientific practice, including how researchers and students verify AI assisted analyses and retain responsibility for analytical decisions.

- [Same data, different analysts](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-024-02101-x) (2025). [PDF]({{ '/papers/Gould_et_al_2025_same_data_different_analysts.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [Data from an international multi centre study of statistics and mathematics anxieties](https://doi.org/10.5334/jopd.80) (2023).
- [Developmental psychologists should care about measurement precision](https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.2321) (2022).
