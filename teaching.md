I teach research methods, statistics, the psychology of AI, and the psychology of language at the University of Hull.

## Research Skills 2

I lead this first year module in quantitative research methods, experimental design, and statistical analysis.

## Psychology of AI

I lead this final year module examining large language models, human cognition, AI agents, trust and responsibility, and the societal implications of AI.

## The Psychology of Language

I teach the word processing block of this final year module, covering word recognition, word learning, and complementary learning systems.

## Research projects

I supervise undergraduate dissertations and coordinate the School's [Research Assistantship Scheme]({{ '/research-assistantship/' | relative_url }}), which pairs undergraduate students with staff on live research projects. Postgraduate supervision is listed on [Research students]({{ '/research-students/' | relative_url }}).
